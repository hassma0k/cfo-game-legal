Privacy Policy

The CFO Game · last updated 13 September 2026

The CFO Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and works offline.

What we store

Your game progress, settings, and purchase status are stored only on your device (in local storage). They are never sent to us or to any third party.

What we do not do

We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies, and the app makes no network requests to our servers. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Purchases

The one-time "Pro" unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment information. Restoring a purchase uses Apple's App Store account system.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Not advice

The CFO Game is an illustrative educational simulation and does not provide financial, investment, or accounting advice.
