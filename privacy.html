Privacy Policy

The CFO Game · last updated 16 October 2026

The CFO Game has no accounts, no identifiers and no ads. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is a small set of anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off at any time.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, purchase status, optional profile answers (your role and goal) and any name you type for CFO Cup score lines are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which parts of the game are used and improve it, the app sends a small number of anonymous events — for example "app opened", "decision completed", "report viewed" — together with coarse context such as the app version, platform (iOS, Android or web), the device language, the topic and grade of a decision, and your answers to the optional "role" and "how did you hear about us" questions. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers, and the data is never linked to you or used for tracking or advertising. Statistics are processed by our analytics provider, Aptabase, which does not store IP addresses. You can switch statistics off in Settings → Privacy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of data, no sign-in.

Purchases

The one-time "Pro" unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your payment information.

Web edition

The free browser demo at hassma0k.github.io/cfo-game-legal/play works the same way: progress stays in your browser's local storage and only the anonymous statistics described above are sent.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Not advice · fictional content

The CFO Game is an illustrative educational simulation and does not provide financial, investment, tax, or accounting advice. All companies, characters, names, and figures in it are entirely fictional and used for illustration only; no endorsement or affiliation is implied. It is an independent personal project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any employer or institution.
