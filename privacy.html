Privacy Policy

The CFO Game · last updated 16 October 2026

The CFO Game has no accounts, no identifiers and no ads. Your game progress stays on your device. The app collects no data: nothing about you or your play is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings, purchase status, optional profile answers (your role and goal) and any name you type for CFO Cup score lines are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

No analytics

The app has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, usage, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. If this ever changes, this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated first, and any statistics would be anonymous and could be switched off in Settings.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of data, no sign-in.

Purchases

The one-time "Pro" unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your payment information.

Web edition

The free browser demo at hassma0k.github.io/cfo-game-legal/play works the same way: progress stays in your browser's local storage and no data is collected.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
