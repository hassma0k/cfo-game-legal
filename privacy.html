Privacy Policy

The CFO Game · last updated 28 October 2026

The CFO Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. When you play on your own, your game progress stays on your device and nothing is sent to us. The only data we receive is for team games, and only when you choose to join or run one — described below.

Playing on your own

Your game progress, settings, purchase status and optional profile answers (your role and goal) are stored only on your device (or in your browser's local storage on the web edition). They are never sent to us.

Team games

A team game is created by an organiser who buys a Team Pass. When you join a team game from an invite link, the game sends the following to our database so the organiser and the other players of that game can see the results:

  • the name you type when you join (use a first name or nickname if you prefer);
  • your game results in that game: for each decision, its name, your choice summary, your grade and the value it created, and your final score;
  • a random identifier created by the game on your device, so you can reopen the link and continue. It is not your device's advertising or hardware identifier.

When you organise a team game, we store the game name and settings you choose and a fingerprint (hash) of a private key that stays on your phone; no payment details reach us.

Who sees it: the organiser sees each player's name, progress, decisions and score; players see the names and scores on the game's ranking. Nobody else. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell or share it.

Where and how long: the data is stored with our database provider, Supabase (as our processor). Each team game and all its players' results are deleted automatically 90 days after the game ends. You can ask us to delete your entry sooner by emailing the address below with the game name and the name you used.

No analytics

The app has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. We do not collect your email, contacts, location, usage statistics, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling of data, no sign-in.

Purchases

Pro (a one-time purchase) and Team Pass (a consumable purchase) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment information.

Web edition

The browser edition at hassma0k.github.io/cfo/play works the same way: progress stays in your browser, and only team games send the data described above.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children; team games are intended for adults in work, training or study settings.
