How to play

Pick an industry, set your board guidance, then make one CFO decision per turn. Each choice moves Economic Value Added (EVA) and company value on screen, and a personalised report explains the concept behind every call.

Restoring your Pro purchase

Open the app, reach the Pro screen (finish the free run, or open it from the menu), and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Pro is one-time

The Pro upgrade is a single purchase — not a subscription. It unlocks the full 10-turn game and all 15 topics permanently.
